# KI-avisa working notes

This repository owns the newsroom formerly called Innsyn. Read
`v5/docs/innsyn/ARBEIDSKORT.md`, then the relevant operational sections of
DRIFT.md, REDAKSJON.md and KILDER.md. The internal `innsyn` ID is retained so
existing cases, source IDs and receipts remain stable.

Use `python agenthouse.py` with the sibling AgentHouse checkout. It selects
AgentHouse's existing `.venv`, shared skills and central model resolver while
keeping cases and outputs here. Read `docs/AGENTHOUSE.md` and each relevant
AgentHouse `SKILL.md`. Do not duplicate tooling or hard-code models in prompts.

KI-avisa and KI-partiet have a common owner. Reporting decisions and evidence
thresholds are independent of the policy programme; scrutinize KI-partiet by
the same method. Disclose the relationship where relevant. Newsroom contact
authority covers ordinary reporting, not political lobbying or submissions.
Do not transfer private correspondence or source identities to KI-partiet.

Check run/status receipts and existing sends before action. Use the standing
journalistic mandate and connected Gmail tools for necessary correspondence;
do not assign reporting to the owner. Preserve exact sent content/provider IDs.

Exception: permission to use the owner's existing UiO/library access is not
permission to contact UiO, its library/e-resource staff, publishers or access
providers about that access, account, licence, automation or newsroom use.
Such contact can identify the owner even without a username. Obtain the owner's
explicit approval for the specific recipient and message before sending any
such inquiry, follow-up or correction. Do not disclose the owner's affiliation,
access route, account details or usage in ordinary reporting correspondence.
Do not infer this approval from the standing journalistic mandate or from a
request to find material through UiO. Continue authorized read-only research
without turning access gaps into unsolicited support requests.

Silence is not an admission. Keep facts, calculations and interpretations clear.
Read originals, preserve contrary evidence and obtain actual opportunities to
reply where required. Stop at real waiting and date the next action.

Private archives are ignored under `v5/workspace/`. Never commit raw mail,
private identities, licensed papers, run logs, media or local sender settings.
Only deliberately prepared public article fields may reach the site. Public
launch still needs the established legal editor/contact arrangements. No
publishing, deployment, film or upload is implicit in research or migration.

The old AgentHouse archive paths are compatibility junctions. Do new work in
this repository. Do not rewrite historical sent receipts or repeat completed
runs after moving. Durable context comes from current briefs, case decisions,
contact logs, handoffs and receipts, not earlier chats or session logs.

Prefer simple solutions, channel-specific guidance in this project and shared
code in AgentHouse. Use the smallest meaningful validation; fail clearly when
required inputs/tools are missing. Operator background is not editorial signal.
